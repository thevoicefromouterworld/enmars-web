Technical Briefing

Every project begins with a structured technical briefing designed to define scope, quantities, timelines and production specifications with clarity. During this phase, ENMARS works closely with partners to understand the operational requirements behind each sampling program, ensuring that format, materials and finishing expectations are aligned from the start.

By establishing precise parameters early in the process, we reduce uncertainty and create a solid foundation for efficient coordination.

Supplier Sourcing

ENMARS selects and aligns specialised from our trusted suppliers according to the technical needs of each project. Rather than relying on a single production source, we coordinate a network of trusted partners, ensuring that every stage — from component sourcing to finishing — is managed through a structured and transparent workflow.

Our centralised communication model provides partners with clear visibility and reduces operational friction, allowing projects to progress with consistency and predictable timelines.

Component Manufacturing

Once specifications are confirmed, production of glass vials and associated elements is carried out by certified manufacturing partners. ENMARS supervises this phase to ensure that components meet industrial standards and remain aligned with project requirements.

Through careful coordination and process oversight, we help maintain material consistency and minimize production risks before assembly begins.

Filling & Assembly

Handling and assembly workflows are mainly managed in ENMARS facilities, ensuring that production stages move forward in a controlled and structured manner. In case needed, we coordinate with other partners for filling and assembly, maintaining clear communication across every step.

This approach allows brands and laboratories to execute sampling programs with confidence, supported by a coordinated process designed to balance precision and scalability.

Wrapping & Finishing

Final presentation plays a critical role in premium sampling programs. ENMARS coordinates wrapping, labeling and finishing processes to ensure that each vial meets both aesthetic, regulatory compliance and technical expectations.

By aligning finishing stages with production timelines, we help maintain consistency across batches while preparing units for distribution in a structured and efficient way.

Quality Flow & Delivery

Quality control is integrated throughout the production workflow rather than treated as a final checkpoint. ENMARS monitors batch consistency, process alignment and partner communication to ensure reliable outcomes and minimize unexpected delays.

Once production is completed, we coordinate delivery logistics to support launches, campaigns and ongoing sampling programs — providing partners with clear visibility and dependable execution from start to finish.
