Solutions

ENMARS delivers precision-engineered sampling solutions centered on the low-ML vial format, ranging from 1.5 ml to 10 ml, designed to support the evolving needs of the fragrance and cosmetics industry. We help brands, laboratories and partners execute sampling programs with clarity, consistency and industrial precision. Our approach focuses on structured workflows, reliable timelines and high-quality finishing, enabling projects to scale while maintaining a refined and professional presentation.

Beyond our core vial solutions, ENMARS also supports bespoke perfumery initiatives and custom projects developed on demand. From special editions and discovery sets to tailored sampling concepts, we collaborate closely with partners to translate creative ideas into structured, production-ready solutions. Our role is to simplify complexity, offering a flexible yet disciplined framework that adapts to unique project requirements while preserving the operational rigor expected from a European industrial partner.
