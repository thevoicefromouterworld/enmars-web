01The problem

Making samples is easy. Making them pay off and arrive on time is not.

Small runs

Cost per vial shoots up

Producing exactly what you need each time is expensive and means repeating the whole process again and again.

Large runs

Stock sits idle

To bring the cost down you produce in one go, and the warehouse fills up with samples for months.

Lead times

They arrive late where it matters

Production rarely lines up with launches, gift-with-purchase and in-store replenishment.

At ENMARS we treat cost and supply as one problem. And we solve it with you.
